# Premium

Wardnet is a complete privacy gateway on its own: ad and tracker
blocking, per-device VPN routing, network zones, local DNS, DHCP, and
device discovery all run self-hosted, on your own hardware, with no
account required.

Premium adds a remote and mobile layer on top, strictly opt-in, never
required to run the gateway itself:

- **Dynamic DNS**, reach your gateway with no domain of your own.
- **Secure remote tunneling**, encrypted inbound access from anywhere,
  with automatic HTTPS. See [remote access](/docs/remote-access).
- **Private DNS**, your phone resolves through your gateway everywhere,
  at home and on cellular, with no VPN. Your filtering and your query
  log follow it off the LAN. See [private DNS](/docs/private-dns).
- **Personal VPN**, run your own inbound WireGuard server so your phone
  and laptop connect back to your home network from anywhere. See
  [personal VPN](/docs/personal-vpn).
- **Both mobile apps**, the User PWA and the Admin mobile PWA. See
  [mobile apps](/docs/mobile-apps).

## Getting Premium

Premium is managed from your Wardnet account, not from the gateway's
own admin site. Create or sign in to an account at
[account.wardnet.network](https://account.wardnet.network) to start a
free trial, see current plans, or manage an existing subscription. The
same account is what you enroll with when setting up
[remote access](/docs/remote-access) from the gateway.
